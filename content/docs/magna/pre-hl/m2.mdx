## Overview

You have the choice of farming Light or Dark first.
I highly recommend farming Dark first as Light is pretty strong early on and will usually have an easier time to farm Avatar.

## Setup

<Setup slug="pre-hl/m2"></Setup>

I recommend setting up a party like this. Once you have farmed Avatar enough, you can slot Abyss Spines over the Claws.

Select Huanglong from the support summon. You are aiming for **~440,000 honors**.
