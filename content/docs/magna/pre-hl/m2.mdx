## Overview

You have the choice of farming Light or Dark first.
I highly recommend farming Avatar first as Light is pretty strong early on and will usually have an easier time to farm Avatar.

      As a newbie, you should probably look at 75% trigger. Metatron gains a buff and you should dispel it as it deals plain damage.
