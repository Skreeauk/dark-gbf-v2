You have the choice of farming Light or Dark first.
I highly recommend farming Dark first as Light is pretty strong early on and will usually have an easier time to farm Avatar.
Once you have farmed Avatar enough, your grid should look something like this.

I recommend setting up a party like this.

Fill up the last slot with anyone. The main weapon can be anything.
Select Huanglong from the support summon. You are aiming to get **~440,000 honors**.
