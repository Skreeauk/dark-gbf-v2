## Overview

The drop rate is pretty low and farming this can be really slow too as not a lot of people do this content (although with the Steam port,
there may be some activity in the raid but it will still be kinda slow).

You can also get this weapon from the Prestige Pendant Shop and Daily Point Shop.
Other limited resources include the Anniversary Weapon Ticket, which gives a full uncap weapon in one go.

For Anima-Animus Core, you’re looking to farm the Agonize.
This weapon improves the skill damage you do. It is recommended to have **2** of these.
