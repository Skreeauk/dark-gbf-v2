## Overview

This raid drops materials to get and uncap Dark Opus weapons to **FLB (4★)**.
If you don’t have a Dark Opus weapon yet, it is highly recommended to get it.

## Setup

The raid has a blue chest with a minimum of **700k honor** however you can still get what you need by leeching.
