At this point, you should already be Rank 170+,
I highly recommend farming Dark Rapture HL / Faa HL first to uncap your Dark Opus weapon to **ULB (5★)** as the blue chest minimum is **~1.2m honors**.

After uncapping your Dark Opus, I also highly recommend farming Belial to get the Chain of Falsehood.

This can make you do Double Strike setups by charge attacking with the Dark Opus as main hand.

This will be the burst setup from now on.

Use characters that do good normal attacks such as Sariel, Seox, Bowman, etc. I recommend asking your friends / crew / discord server for help in selecting which characters to use as this game has a lot of characters.

If you have **Pain and Suffering**, you can replace Abydos with that.

Your rotation will be:

<div className="text-center mx-auto">

Turn 1: Beelzebub + Pandemonium + MC 1st skill + other character skills

Turn 2: Splitting Spirit

Turn 3 (if needed): Celestial Genesis

</div>

This will give you plenty of honors to farm this raid.

Celeste Ater drops useful weapons and materials to transcend your Celeste Omega summon to the next level. Aim for these:

You will need a lot of **Immortal Fragments** to transcend your Omega summons.

It is highly recommended to transcend your Omega summons to get better auras.
