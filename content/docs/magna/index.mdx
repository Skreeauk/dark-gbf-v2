## Getting Started

I highly recommend reading the wiki basic grid guide to learn what to aim for.
I also recommend progressing the Sierokarte’s Academy.
This has a lot of missions for you to do that will accelerate your progress by a lot and also teach the game’s basic functions and gameplay loop.
